    Used only for channels your provider's guide leaves empty. With the Sky layout on, Ferry first fills Sky-matched channels from Sky's own schedule. Add XMLTV feed URLs below (one per line, .xml or .xml.gz) for anything still missing; channels are matched by guide ID or name.

    Sky layout

    Ferry fetches Sky's channel list for your region and matches it to your playlist. Matched channels get their Sky number (101 BBC One, 401 Sky Sports Main Event) and appear in Sky's sections in Sky order; typing a number on the remote tunes it.

    Duplicate channels

    Channels in the same group with the same guide ID and name (ignoring tags like FHD, 720p or backup) become one row. The TV plays the best stream — highest resolution, then frame rate — and falls back to the others if it fails. Hold OK on a channel to pick a stream yourself. Changing this refreshes your playlists.
